# Font licenses

Both families are downloaded by `next/font/google` at build time and served from
this app's own origin (no runtime font CDN).

| Family | Designer | License |
|---|---|---|
| Bricolage Grotesque | Mathieu Triay | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |
| Instrument Sans | Rodrigo Fuenzalida, Jordan Egstad (Instrument) | SIL Open Font License 1.1 |

OFL 1.1 allows use, bundling and redistribution with software, including
commercially, provided the fonts are not sold on their own and the license is
retained. Full text: https://openfontlicense.org/open-font-license-official-text/
